## For printing, the “best” aspect ratio depends on:

* book type
* photo quality
* viewing distance
* whether you want full-page or collage layouts

For most home/office printing and photo books, **A4 is excellent** because it’s standard, easy to print, and works well for both portraits and collages.

---

# A4 Size Basics

## A4 dimensions

### Physical size

* 210 × 297 mm

### Aspect ratio

\frac{210}{297} \approx 1:1.414

That’s the ISO paper ratio used worldwide.

---

# Best image aspect ratios for A4

| Use Case            | Recommended Ratio |
| ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Full-page portrait  | 3:4 or A-series   |
| Full-page landscape | 4:3               |
| Photo book          | 4:5               |
| Social/photo prints | 2:3               |
| AI art/posters      | 16:9              |
| Kids charts/collage | square or 4:5     |

# How many pictures on one A4?

## Best practical layouts

### 1. Two images on A4

BEST for:

* story books
* kids charts
* detailed illustrations
* portrait photos

Layout:

```text id="dhxjci"
┌──────────────┐
│    Image 1   │
├──────────────┤
│    Image 2   │
└──────────────┘
```

Advantages:

* clearer details
* larger faces
* better print quality
* less pixelation

Recommended if images contain:

* text
* facial expressions
* AI art details

---

## 2. Four images on A4

BEST for:

* sticker sheets
* collage pages
* potty charts
* flashcards
* comic panels

Layout:

```text id="e4ztm1"
┌──────┬──────┐
│ Img1 │ Img2 │
├──────┼──────┤
│ Img3 │ Img4 │
└──────┴──────┘
```

Advantages:

* saves paper
* good for quick visual sets
* ideal for toddlers/kids activities

BUT:

* each image becomes smaller
* details may blur if source resolution is low

---

# My recommendation

## Use 2 images per A4 if:

* you want premium print quality
* AI-generated faces are important
* images contain details/text
* making books

## Use 4 images per A4 if:

* cartoon style
* preschool charts
* flashcards
* small printable cards
* simple illustrations

# Best layout for books

## Children’s books

* 1 large image/page
* or 2 panels/page

## Comic books

* 4–6 panels/page

## AI photo books

* 1 image/page preferred

# Professional printing tip

Always generate at:

* 300 DPI equivalent
* high-resolution PNG
* avoid JPEG compression

# Best balance overall

| Layout       | Quality   | Best For                  |
| ------------ | --------- | ------------------------- |
| 1 image/A4   | highest   | posters/books             |
| 2 images/A4  | excellent | storybooks                |
| 4 images/A4  | good      | activity sheets           |
| 6+ images/A4 | average   | thumbnails/contact sheets |

For your preschool-style charts and printable AI art, **4 images per A4** is usually the sweet spot.
